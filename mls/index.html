The residence

Ground floor, no stairs, and sun on the patio.

Most homes at Golden Oaks look across at a neighbor. This one looks out over the pool, the lawn and the landmark oaks, with no view into another home. It is one of the few in the community with an expanded patio, about twenty feet wide, that catches the sun.

Inside, everything is on one level: an open living and dining room with a fireplace, an updated kitchen with stainless appliances, two bedrooms, and a bath with a separate dressing room and walk-in closet. Parking and the laundry are a short walk from the door.

The community

Golden Oaks, under the canopy.

Golden Oaks is a condominium community at 30 Monte Vista Drive, built around a central pool, a clubhouse and a grove of mature coast live oaks. Paths wind between the buildings under the trees, and 2107 looks out on the heart of it.
